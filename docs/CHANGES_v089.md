# Mini Fort Zombie v089: co-op siege control, zombie body rules, pun graves, double doors

Everything in `docs/CHANGES_v088.md` still applies. Co-op players need the same build: the version number moved to 089.

## 1. Co-op: guests at the siege, spectator camera
- **Guests could not fight at the siege.** The siege usually starts straight from the fort (the clock reaching day 14, or ending the day at the HQ). That path called `startSiege()` directly, never `go()`, so guests were never sent the siege world and stayed in the fort world under fort rules (look and run only: no attacks, doors, use). `coop.js` now wraps `startSiege`: the host arms the siege (seed, `start` message, signature) exactly as for other worlds. The guest's fort rules also switch off whenever `G.mode` is `SIEGE`. A guest never starts a siege on its own.
- **Spectator camera inside the player's head.** The render hook that places the follow camera was only installed if the renderer already existed when `coop.js` loaded, and it doesn't (`init()` makes it later). So the camera never moved and sat on the player rig, which the spectator code parks on the watched player. The hook now waits for the renderer. The camera is also placed properly in world space (it is a child of the rig `pl`, so its local transform is now worked out from the rig's world matrix). `resetCam()` puts it back for first-person play when the guest gets a survivor.
- **Mouse wheel zoom** while spectating: 1.6 to 22 m behind the watched player, eased. The camera rises with distance, so far views look down on the player, and it never dips into the floor. The spectator bar mentions it.

## 2. Zombie bodies
- **"Legs vanish when it dies / shooting one leg takes both off".** This was a rendering bug, not damage. The v088 merged zombie mesh (`zbatch.js`) decides whether each part is drawn by walking up from the part to the mesh's parent. The death ragdoll and the crawl pose re-parent everything hanging on the zombie root (except the legs) onto the pelvis, and that took the merged mesh with it. From then on the legs never met that parent on the way up, counted as "detached" and collapsed. The walk now stops at the zombie root, and the ragdoll and `crawlKids` leave the merged mesh on the root. Checked headless: before the fix one leg shot or one kill hid both legs; after it, only the cut limb is hidden.
- **Which part was hit.** Shots and blows used the hit mesh's own `userData.part`, so outfit pieces, boots, hoods and hair counted as the torso. `zPartOf(mesh, zombie)` (`08_fortworld.js`) now falls back to the limb pivot the mesh hangs under. Shots, corpse shots and aimed melee use it.
- **New death rules (`gore.js`).** A zombie dies in one of only two ways:
  - **Head removed.** This happens on a critical, or when the killing blow lands on the head. The zombie becomes a headless corpse that can be put back together (any head fits, as before) and stood up by medics. Every corpse still in one piece is now revivable (`u.revive = true`).
  - **Torso destroyed.** The killing blow lands on the torso, or comes from damage with no body part (spikes, fire, explosions, survivors' hits on the torso). The body bursts and its head and remaining limbs drop as loose parts that medics can graft onto other bodies.
  - Arm and leg hits take that limb off but can never kill (the damage stops 1 hp short).
  - The stealth kill takes the head.
  - Scripted deaths (Dev Mode, the staged medic scene) keep their plain corpse.
  - In co-op the rules run on the host only; guests get the results as the existing limb, kill and gib events.
  - New: `MFZGORE.lethal(z, part, from)` for kills decided outside `hitZ` / `dmgZ` (the spikes use it).

## 3. Co-op weather
The host's current weather preset (`RGATM.name`) rides on every snapshot (`wx`). A guest shows it through `RGATM._coop`, which `atmosphere.js` checks before its own choice. It snaps on the first snapshot in each world and blends after that.

## 4. Options > Quit to Main Menu
The button shows only when Options was opened from a game, and asks for confirmation first. Anything since the last save or autosave is lost, and in co-op it ends the session. It reloads the page and goes straight to the main menu (it skips the title screen; `sessionStorage` `mfz_toMenu`). The reload clears the world, the campaign and any connection completely.

## 5. Cemetery headstones
Every standing stone has its own pun name, years and a short epitaph, and none repeat. There are 116 names: the 47 supplied (the duplicate Chris P. Bacon was used once) plus horror and pop-culture puns. A cemetery uses about 106. Toppled and broken stones and the burst-open graves carry no plate. The plates are cells of one 2048 × 2048 atlas (one material): a near-black rounded plate with light lettering, a black text shadow and a gold epitaph line. Each is sized to the face it sits on, so the text is never squashed. If a future layout has more standing stones than names, the extra stones are left blank instead of repeating a name (with a console warning). `MFZCEM.used()` reports the count.

## 6 and 7. Building fixes
- **Aisle Be Back:** the white chevron over each portal door had its front face exactly on the stone gable's bevelled face (both at z 0.34), so the two z-fought in a triangle. The chevron is now 4 cm proud of the gable.
- **Dead Weight:** the projecting entrance bay was one full-height block that walled over the doors. It is now a header over a 2.6 m opening on two piers, which is what its collision always was. The bay has a soffit, and the Little League poster moved onto the shop front.

## 8. Double doors
- Mark two neighbouring door ops in a `WL` wall with the same `dbl` (`'swing'` or `'slide'`) to make them one double door. The leaves are hinged on the outer edges and linked (`d.pair`): opening or closing either one opens or closes both, and opening the pair unlocks it.
- Each leaf keeps its own hit points. Zombies, shots and blows break them down one leaf at a time, like any other door.
- `'slide'` leaves are glass shop doors with an aluminium frame, push bar and safety strip. They run on a track just inside the wall and slide 1.27 m apart along it instead of swinging (`d.slide`, `d.lf`; the main loop animates `lf.position.x`). Their open box is where the leaf ends up.
- Skins and interior linings still see two plain `'d'` openings, so nothing else in the layers changed.
- **Where:**
  - Swinging pairs: church tower portal, cinema front and both auditorium entrances, bowling front, Bruno's Burgerama front, supermarket back room.
  - Sliding glass: supermarket (Food Library), Morning Wood Hardware and Dead Weight fronts.
- Co-op: door ids are unchanged (one per leaf). A guest opening one leaf opens both on every screen.

## Tested (headless Chromium, software GL)
- Zombie rules (fort world, real `hitZ` / `dmgZ` / `melee`): a 500-damage leg shot takes one leg and the zombie lives (the other leg is still drawn). A lethal head hit leaves a headless revivable corpse with both legs drawn. A lethal torso hit gibs the body and drops 5 loose parts. Plain damage gibs. Arm, arm and leg hits of 999 each leave the zombie alive at 1.5 hp. The stealth kill takes the head. An outfit mesh under a leg pivot resolves to `legL`. The same test on v088 hid both legs after one leg shot or a kill.
- Double doors: church (swing) and Dead Weight (slide) pairs open and close together and swap the right solids. Breaking one leaf leaves the other working. A town build has 5 pairs (4 sliding leaves). Renders of every changed front, closed and open, plus an interior view of the sliding track.
- Lot check: every changed lot builds with no errors. The cinema's `computeBoundingSphere NaN` warning is the same on v088.
- Co-op (scripted stand-in): `startSiege()` from the fort on a host sends `start` (mode SIEGE) and `sig`. A fort guest's keys pass in SIEGE and are still swallowed in FORT_BUILD. The spectator camera sits 4.5 m behind and 2.6 m up, zooms to 13.8 m and back to 1.6 m with the wheel, looks at the target, and returns to the rig.
